How an engagement begins

First understand.
Then decide.

I don't believe in prescribing a workshop or redesign before we've understood the problem. These early steps help us decide whether and how to work together.

01Share the context

Tell me what you're trying to change, who's affected and what you've already tried.

02Explore the challenge

We discuss goals, constraints, existing evidence and what a useful outcome could look like.

03Agree a direction

If there's a fit, I'll propose a sensible scope, deliverables and next steps. No commitment is created by sending an enquiry.

A few practical questions

Before we begin.

Can I book a time immediately?

Use the booking request to share your preferred date and time. I'll reply to confirm a mutually suitable slot. The form does not reserve live calendar availability.

Do you work with early-stage founders as well as enterprises?

Yes. The approach and scale depend on the problem. A founder may need help framing an initial proposition; an established team may need an independent UX assessment or a more complex service-design conversation.

How are engagements priced?

Scopes differ in research depth, stakeholders, delivery time and the degree of technical investigation required. I’ll discuss a suitable engagement structure and fees after understanding what you need.

Will you work on confidential material?

We can discuss confidentiality and appropriate handling before any sensitive client information is shared. Please avoid placing confidential records or credentials in the initial enquiry form.

Have something in mind?

Tell me what you're working through.

You don't need a perfect brief. A description of the situation is enough to start.
