Why these ideas exist.

Expand each concept for its problem, my contribution and the learning so far.

01 · RevetaA venture ecosystem for human-centred intelligent products+

The challenge is turning ambitious technology ideas into focused, usable products. My role spans strategy, UX direction and implementation planning. The learning is to validate each proposition before expanding the platform.

02 · X-huntCommunity participation and coordination+

People struggle to find meaningful ways to contribute, while organisations struggle to mobilise skills. I have led research, concept framing and product design. The work reinforces the importance of trust, relevance and recognition.

03 · AuraAn intelligent research and creation workspace+

AI interfaces can become cluttered when tools, conversations and outputs compete for attention. My work covers product direction and UX strategy. The lesson is to make complexity manageable through progressive disclosure.

04 · Landmark AIProperty trust and verification+

Fragmented evidence makes property decisions harder to trust. I have contributed to concept and experience architecture. The opportunity is to distinguish verified evidence from unverified claims.

05 · Navigator XEvidence-led product development+

Teams can create polished artefacts without preserving the reasoning behind them. My work explores research-connected workflows and UX architecture. The goal is more traceable decisions.

06 · OASIXImmersive XR storytelling+

Environments can become part of a narrative rather than passive backdrops. My role involves spatial concept development and experience design. The key tension is immersion versus clarity and accessibility.
