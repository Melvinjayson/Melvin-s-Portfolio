01 / Context

Knowledge needs context.

Healthcare teams navigate specialised information, evolving procedures and complex decisions. The experience challenge was to make relevant information easier to discover and interpret without removing professional judgement from the process.

02 / Design contribution

Clarity before automation.

The UX direction centred on knowledge pathways, interaction architecture and the role of AI-assisted discovery. The design principle was to support confident human decisions rather than hide complexity behind opaque answers.

UX lens / Design reasoning

The question behind the interface.

How can a specialist find reliable information without mistaking AI output for an authoritative source?

01 · Human need

Understand the user

Provenance, confidence and progressive disclosure. The interface should reflect how people interpret information, recognise risk and decide what to do next.

02 · Experience strategy

Structure the interaction

Knowledge architecture, contextual retrieval, review checkpoints. This is an interpretation of the design approach rather than a claim that every technique was used in a documented research session.

03 · Design principle

Make the outcome legible

Evidence-linked answers and understandable next actions. The experience should help people understand the state of the system and retain meaningful control.
