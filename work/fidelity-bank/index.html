What needed to change?

Digital banking is more than a collection of screens. Customers need to understand financial choices, navigate uncertainty and complete everyday tasks without unnecessary effort.

The design contribution involved product experience leadership, user-centred journey thinking and coherent interaction patterns across financial workflows. Accessibility, confidence and clarity were important design considerations.

03 / Design rationale

Why these decisions matter.

A useful banking interface anticipates moments of hesitation: fees, status, confirmations and error recovery. The experience should communicate what has happened and what users can safely do next.

01 / The brief beneath the brief

What I was really trying to solve.

Banking interfaces make trust tangible. A customer needs to understand what they are authorising, whether it succeeded and what to do if something does not look right. I was responsible for helping the team rethink retail and corporate online banking journeys.

02 / My contribution

What I worked through.

I led UX work with business, engineering and delivery colleagues, drawing on user research and testing to improve flows and translate design decisions into implemented interfaces.

03 / Reflection

What stayed with me.

I learned that good financial design is as much about reassurance and recovery as it is about speed. The moments between submitting a transaction and receiving confirmation deserve as much care as the happy path.

My 2026 CV reports improvements in satisfaction and digital transaction volume; attribution and measurement methodology are not independently documented here.

UX lens / Design reasoning

The question behind the interface.

How can digital banking feel clear and trustworthy for customers with different levels of financial confidence?

01 · Human need

Understand the user

Accessibility, trust and transactional feedback. The interface should reflect how people interpret information, recognise risk and decide what to do next.

02 · Experience strategy

Structure the interaction

Journey simplification, predictable controls and transparent confirmation. This is an interpretation of the design approach rather than a claim that every technique was used in a documented research session.

03 · Design principle

Make the outcome legible

A banking experience that explains each important state. The experience should help people understand the state of the system and retain meaningful control.
