The challenge

Beyond the brief.

The interesting question in autonomous systems is not whether software can make a decision. It is whether an operator can understand what the system is doing, know when to intervene and retain responsibility.

The design work

What I focused on.

I explored operator-centred interaction patterns for decision visibility, human intervention, authority boundaries and traceability as part of a proposal and architecture exercise.

UX lens / Design reasoning

The question behind the interface.

How can operators understand and govern delegated machine decisions?

01 · Human need

Understand the user

Human oversight, explainability and safe intervention. The interface should reflect how people interpret information, recognise risk and decide what to do next.

02 · Experience strategy

Structure the interaction

Decision visibility, authority boundaries and traceable actions. This is an interpretation of the design approach rather than a claim that every technique was used in a documented research session.

03 · Design principle

Make the outcome legible

A proposal-stage framework for governed autonomy, not a deployed system. The experience should help people understand the state of the system and retain meaningful control.
