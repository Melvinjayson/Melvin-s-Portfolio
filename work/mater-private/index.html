01 / Context and design challenge

What needed to change?

Healthcare services can feel complicated before a patient even arrives. People may need to compare services, understand availability and take the next step while managing uncertainty.

02 / Approach and contribution

Making complexity legible.

The work addressed digital patient journeys, service clarity and the interactions that connect discovery with appointment booking. The design emphasis was on removing avoidable friction while retaining reassurance and trust.

03 / Design rationale

Why these decisions matter.

Designing for healthcare means respecting the emotional context of the journey. Clear language, predictable steps and accessible feedback are as important as conversion.

04 / Outcomes, limitations and reflection

Impact with context.

Professional materials report a 22% bookings uplift within the engagement; the public summary does not independently isolate the contribution of specific UX changes.
