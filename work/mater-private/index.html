01 / Context and design challenge

What needed to change?

Healthcare services can feel complicated before a patient even arrives. People may need to compare services, understand availability and take the next step while managing uncertainty.

02 / Approach and contribution

Making complexity legible.

The work addressed digital patient journeys, service clarity and the interactions that connect discovery with appointment booking. The design emphasis was on removing avoidable friction while retaining reassurance and trust.

03 / Design rationale

Why these decisions matter.

Designing for healthcare means respecting the emotional context of the journey. Clear language, predictable steps and accessible feedback are as important as conversion.

UX lens / Design reasoning

The question behind the interface.

How can people move from seeking care to booking it with less uncertainty?

01 · Human need

Understand the user

Service journeys, reassurance and accessibility. The interface should reflect how people interpret information, recognise risk and decide what to do next.

02 · Experience strategy

Structure the interaction

Information hierarchy, clearer appointment pathways and helpful guidance. This is an interpretation of the design approach rather than a claim that every technique was used in a documented research session.

03 · Design principle

Make the outcome legible

A more comprehensible route to the next step. The experience should help people understand the state of the system and retain meaningful control.
