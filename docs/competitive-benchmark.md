## 2. Third-party hiring commentary (not official)

- A widely shared hiring-manager post (LinkedIn, 2026, reposted by many designers) asks for 2–4 strongest cases on the homepage, one click to a real case, output and impact first, scannable structure, and states that vague roles ("we did…"), missing outcomes and polished screens without decisions are reasons to reject. At Staff level it expects at least one case that "clearly moved a meaningful business metric".
- A widely shared 2026 LinkedIn post (David Hildebrand) warns enterprise designers against leading with AI chatbot case studies, because chat is rarely the centre of gravity for enterprise product teams. The rebuild leads with workflows and boundaries, not chat UIs.
- NN/g's survey of 200+ hiring managers emphasises scannability and that full portfolios are rarely read word for word.
- Interview accounts for Google product design describe the portfolio review as "not a showcase, it is an interview", probing personal ownership and trade-offs (interviewquery.com, third-party).

## 4. Patterns adopted

1. **Specialism sentence first** (Chen, Ritacca) → hero H1.
2. **Role, scope and status on every card** (Roberts, Barilla) → capability + domain meta and evidence badge.
3. **Résumé one click from anywhere** (Roberts, Barilla) → nav item + footer + PDF.
4. **System as proof** (Ritacca, Lovin) → Lab section "The portfolio is a product too".
5. **Text, not logos, for employers and clients** → removed favicon wall.
6. **Real product first** (Kipin, Nelson) → X-hunt leads, with light/dark renders of its actual interface.
7. **Factual status lines, not hedges** (Barilla, Nelson) → cards read "Live pilot · 2026 · Co-founder"; evidence badges moved inside case outcomes.
8. **AI-assisted building as a skill, shown honestly** (Kipin) → X-hunt states that most commits are co-authored with AI agents and that the guard-rails are the design work.

## 5. Patterns deliberately not adopted

- Logo rows of employers/clients (risk of implied endorsement; several previous logos had no explainable relationship).
- Looping video headers (performance, and no shippable footage exists to use).
- Availability ticker / "open to roles" banner (sensitive while employed; keep in direct outreach instead).
